Box League Scoring and Standings

Choose a points system for your club box league, settle tied standings and handle unfinished fixtures with a worked example.

Published 26 November 2026

Agree how league points work before the first match. A player should be able to work out where they stand from the results and the published rules.

Separate match scores from league points

The score records what happened on court. League points decide the standings. A simple club system could award three league points for a win, one for a played loss and zero for an unplayed match. That gives players a reason to complete fixtures even when they are unlikely to win. If you prefer wins alone to determine the table, award one point for a win and zero for a loss.

Use a worked example to check your table

In a four-player box, Asha wins twice and loses once. She earns 7 points (3 + 3 + 1). Ben also wins twice and loses once, for 7 points. If your first tie-break for two players is head-to-head and Ben beat Asha, Ben finishes above her.

Write the tie-break order down

For example, order by league points, then wins, then head-to-head when exactly two players remain tied. For a three-way tie, use a measure you can calculate consistently, such as games won minus games lost, and specify what happens if players are still level. Confirm that your league software can apply the chosen order, or tell players how the organiser will resolve ties at the end of the round.

Handle walkovers and withdrawals consistently

Mark a walkover separately from a played score. Decide whether a walkover earns league points and how it affects game difference. Set a policy for withdrawals, such as removing all the withdrawn player’s fixtures from the table. Apply the same policy to every box so opponents are treated consistently.

Check the table before publishing the next round

Keep a record of the fixture, score and result status. Give players a short window to flag errors, correct disputed entries with both players, then freeze the final standings before promotion and relegation. Use the match scheduling guide to catch unplayed fixtures before the deadline.
