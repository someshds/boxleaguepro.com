Set up your club

Running a tennis, padel, squash, racketball or badminton box league? About 2 minutes. Your club is set up automatically, usually within minutes, and we email you the link.

Loading form…
What happens next:
  1. BoxLeague Pro sets your club up automatically — usually within minutes. Some requests are held for a quick review.
  2. We email you a dedicated link (e.g. ?club=your-club)
  3. Sign in and follow the on-screen guide to create a league, choose scoring and add players
  4. Share the club link through your preferred channel so players can sign in and add availability
Tell us about your club in the Message field:
  • Club name & location (e.g. "East Grinstead Tennis Club, West Sussex")
  • Sport (tennis / padel / squash / racketball / badminton)
  • How many leagues you want to run to start with
  • Court booking system URL if you have one (ClubSpark, Playtomic, etc.)
  • Anything else we should know
